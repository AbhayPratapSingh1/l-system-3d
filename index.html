L-System 3D

Interactive 3D L-systems (Lindenmayer systems) in the browser: trees, bushes, plants, pines, a 3D Hilbert curve, seashells, a dragon curve, roots, coral, lightning and vines. Each one is grown from a grammar and drawn by a 3D turtle. Use the keyboard to switch L-systems, change iterations, turn angle, branch length and radius, and move the camera.
